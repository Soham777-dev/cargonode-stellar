# CargoNode User Onboarding Guide

**Welcome to CargoNode Testing!**

Thank you for helping test CargoNode, a decentralized freight logistics payment platform on Stellar. This guide will walk you through setting up your wallet and creating your first shipment.

---

## What You'll Need

- **5-10 minutes** of your time
- **Google Chrome or Firefox** browser (recommended)
- **Freighter Wallet** (we'll help you install it)

---

## Step 1: Install Freighter Wallet (2 minutes)

Freighter is a browser extension wallet for Stellar. It's like MetaMask for Stellar.

### Installation:

1. **Go to**: [https://www.freighter.app/](https://www.freighter.app/)
2. Click **"Add to Chrome"** (or Firefox)
3. Click **"Add Extension"** when prompted
4. You should see the Freighter icon (🚀) in your browser toolbar

### Create Your Wallet:

1. Click the Freighter icon in your browser
2. Click **"Create New Wallet"**
3. **IMPORTANT**: Write down your 12-word recovery phrase and store it safely
4. Set a password for your wallet
5. Click **"Confirm"**

✅ **Done!** You now have a Stellar wallet.

---

## Step 2: Switch to Testnet (1 minute)

We're testing on Stellar **Testnet** (not real money).

1. Click the Freighter icon
2. Click the **Settings** gear icon (⚙️)
3. Scroll down to **"Network"**
4. Select **"Testnet"**
5. Click **"Save"**

✅ Your wallet is now on Testnet!

---

## Step 3: Get Testnet XLM (1 minute)

You need testnet XLM for transaction fees (it's free!).

1. In Freighter, click your address to copy it (starts with `G...`)
2. Go to: [https://laboratory.stellar.org/#account-creator](https://laboratory.stellar.org/#account-creator)
3. Paste your address
4. Click **"Get test network lumens"**
5. Wait 5-10 seconds

✅ You should now see 10,000 XLM in Freighter!

---

## Step 4: Get Testnet USDC (Contact Us)

To create shipments, you need test USDC tokens.

**Contact the CargoNode team** (via Discord/Telegram/Email) with:
- Your Stellar testnet address (starts with `G...`)
- We'll send you test USDC tokens within 24 hours

---

## Step 5: Access CargoNode (30 seconds)

1. Go to: **[https://cargonode-stellar.vercel.app/](https://cargonode-stellar.vercel.app/)**
2. You should see the CargoNode homepage

---

## Step 6: Connect Your Wallet (30 seconds)

1. On the CargoNode homepage, click **"Connect Wallet"**
2. Freighter will pop up asking for permission
3. Click **"Connect"**

✅ Your wallet is now connected! You should see your address in the top-right corner.

---

## Step 7: Create Your First Shipment (3 minutes)

Now let's create a test shipment!

1. Click **"New Shipment"** button
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Shipper Address**: Your address (should auto-fill)
   - **Driver Address**: Use any other Stellar address (or ask team for test address)
   - **Amount**: `100` USDC
   - **Origin**: `Mumbai`
   - **Destination**: `Delhi`
   - **Cargo Description**: `Electronics - Test Shipment`
   - **Cargo Weight**: `500` kg

3. Click **"Create Shipment"**
4. Freighter will pop up asking you to sign the transaction
5. Click **"Approve"**
6. Wait 3-5 seconds for confirmation

✅ **Success!** You've created your first shipment on Stellar!

---

## Step 8: View Your Shipment (30 seconds)

1. Click **"Shipments"** in the navigation
2. You should see your shipment listed
3. Click on it to see details

---

## What Happens Next?

As a **Shipper**, you created a shipment and locked USDC in escrow.

Now a **Driver** would:
1. See available shipments
2. Accept your shipment
3. Pick up the cargo
4. Deliver it

Then you (Shipper) would:
1. Confirm delivery
2. The smart contract automatically releases payment to the driver

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Transaction Failed"
- **Check**: Do you have testnet XLM for fees?
- **Check**: Do you have enough test USDC?
- **Solution**: Contact the team for help

### "Wallet Not Connecting"
- **Try**: Refresh the page
- **Try**: Disconnect and reconnect in Freighter settings
- **Check**: Is Freighter set to Testnet?

### "Can't See My Shipment"
- **Wait**: Stellar transactions take 3-5 seconds
- **Try**: Refresh the shipments page
- **Check**: Is your wallet still connected?
